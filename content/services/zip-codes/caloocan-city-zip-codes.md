## How to format your mailing address

Place the ZIP code on the city line of your address, right before the region and country.

**Example:**

```
Juan dela Cruz
Unit 5, Liberty Building, Tamban St.
Kaunlaran Village, Caloocan City
1409 Metro Manila
Philippines
```

For domestic mail within the Philippines, you may omit the country line. Unit number, building/house name, and subdivision are optional.
