## Full Disclosure Policy (Department of the Interior and Local Government)

The Full Disclosure Policy requires LGUs to publish the following every quarter:

- **Budget and Financial Accountability Reports**
- **Bids and Awards Information**
- **Supplemental and Annual Budgets**
- **Statement of Debt and Receipts**

All FDP documents are also posted on the DILG-run transparency portal.
